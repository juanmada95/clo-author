# Annotated Bibliography — Teacher Digital Competence (TDC) in Initial Teacher Training in Spain

**Project:** Curricular analysis of TDC in *Grado en Maestro de Educación Infantil* and *Grado en Maestro de Educación Primaria* in Spain, mapped against DigCompEdu (Redecker & Punie, 2017) and the Spanish MRCDD (Resolución 4 mayo 2022, BOE-A-2022-8042).

**Date compiled:** 2026-04-26
**Round 2 revisions:** 2026-04-26 (see changelog at bottom)
**Total entries:** 54 (Round 1: 51; +5 added; -1 dropped; Bordón 76(3) entry resolved as 78(1) 2026)
**Search scope:** 2015–2026 (with foundational references regardless of date); ES + EN; top international + Iberoamerican journals; institutional and legal documents.

**Citation honesty conventions:**
- Entries marked `[VERIFIED]` were cross-checked against publisher pages, DOIs or BOE links during search.
- Entries marked `[PARTIAL VERIFICATION]` had confirmed title + journal + author but page numbers or DOI couldn't be fully checked.
- Entries marked `[UNVERIFIED]` were referenced indirectly (citation chains) and could not be cross-checked. Treat with caution and verify before citing.

## Bucket A — Curricular analysis of teacher training programs (highest scooping risk)

### A1. Cuevas-Monzonís, N., Tabuenca-Cuevas, M., et al. (2025). *Digital competencies in the EFL classroom: What is (or isn't) part of the curriculum for pre-service primary EFL teachers at Spanish universities?* **The Curriculum Journal**. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1002/curj.70053]
- **Question:** Which DigCompEdu competences appear in the EFL course syllabi of the *Grado en Maestro de Primaria* across Spanish public universities?
- **Method:** Document analysis of course guides (*guías docentes*).
- **Sample:** 39 Spanish public universities; 136 EFL-related courses (58 compulsory + 78 optional) in Primary degrees.
- **Findings:** Heterogeneous coverage; digital competence mostly handled as a generic transversal competency rather than tailored to FLT; significant gaps relative to DigCompEdu.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — direct scooping risk.** This is methodologically the closest paper to the proposed project (document analysis of Spanish *guías docentes* mapped against DigCompEdu in Primary degrees). The escape valve is that Cuevas et al. restrict to the **EFL subset** within the Primary degree; the project differentiates by analyzing the **whole degree** plus **Infantil** plus the *memorias de verificación* layer.

### A2. Pradas-Esteban, B., & Tabuenca-Cuevas, M. (2024). *Digital competence training of EFL primary pre-service teachers: A systematic review of the Spanish context.* **Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 13**(5), 357–370. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** What does the empirical literature say about digital competence training of EFL primary pre-service teachers in Spanish universities?
- **Method:** Systematic literature review.
- **Findings:** Few Spain-focused studies; identified an explicit gap calling for a national-level analysis of university curricula referenced to DigCompEdu — i.e., the gap that A1 then partially fills.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Same author cluster, same context, complementary scope (review feeding A1).

### A3. Cuevas-Monzonís, N., et al. (2024). *ICT integration in FLT: An analysis of TPACK implementation in Spanish Primary Teacher Education.* **Digital Education Review**. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** How is TPACK reflected in FLT *guías docentes* of Spanish Primary teacher training?
- **Method:** Content analysis of 496 course guides across 39 Spanish public universities.
- **Findings:** Less than half of subjects incorporate technology; TPACK presence is moderate; technology often appears as a broad competency rather than FLT-specific.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — same data, different framework.** Same corpus as A1 but uses TPACK rather than DigCompEdu. Strong scooping risk because the *memoria-vs-guía-docente* methodological move overlaps.

### A4. Sanz-Benito, I., Lázaro-Cantabrana, J. L., & Grimalt-Álvaro, C. (2024). *La formación de competencias en inclusión digital en los grados de Educación Infantil y Primaria de las universidades españolas: una necesidad aún por cubrir.* **Educar, 60**(2), 321–335. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** To what extent do Spanish *Grado en Maestro de Infantil* and *Primaria* curricula develop competences for digital inclusion?
- **Method:** Document analysis of degree curricula across Spanish universities, focused on the inclusion subset.
- **Findings:** Digital inclusion competences are not explicitly integrated; coverage is fragmentary.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — direct competitor on the same degrees.** Same target population (Infantil + Primaria across Spain). Differentiator: Sanz-Benito et al. focus on the **inclusion** sub-area; a comprehensive DigCompEdu / MRCDD curricular mapping is still open.

### A5. Peirats Chacón, J., Marín Suelves, D., Granados Saiz, J., & Morote Blanco, D. (2018). *Competencia digital en los planes de estudios de universidades públicas españolas.* **REDU. Revista de Docencia Universitaria, 16**(1). [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** How is digital competence addressed in the *planes de estudios* of teacher-training degrees at Spanish (Valencian Community + others) public universities?
- **Method:** Document analysis of *planes de estudios*.
- **Findings:** Limited specific ICT credits; scarce presence as a transversal competence except in ICT specializations.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — direct precedent.** Earliest Spanish curricular analysis on this question; pre-MRCDD; smaller geographic scope. Differentiator: it predates both MRCDD (2022) and the current DigCompEdu instantiation, and uses *planes de estudio* rather than the more granular *memorias de verificación* + *guías docentes*.

### A6. Granados Saiz, J., Peirats Chacón, J., et al. (2020). *Profesorado y TIC: análisis de los planes de estudio en universidades españolas.* **Aula de Encuentro, 27**(1), 146–172. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** How are ICT incorporated in the *planes de estudios* of teacher-training degrees (Infantil, Primaria) and *Máster en Profesorado* in Spanish universities?
- **Method:** Quantitative descriptive document analysis of *planes de estudios*.
- **Findings:** Heterogeneous implementation; most universities relegate ICT to optional courses.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — direct precedent.** Companion to A5 with broader institutional coverage. Same methodological limitation: works at the *plan de estudios* level, not at the *memoria* + *guía docente* + DigCompEdu mapping level.

### A7. Sandvik, M., Smørdal, O., & Østerud, S. (2023). *Digital competence and digital technology: A curriculum analysis of Norwegian early childhood teacher education.* **Scandinavian Journal of Educational Research**. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1080/00313831.2023.2204109]
- **Question:** How are digital competence and digital technology addressed at ideological / formal / perceived curriculum levels in Norwegian ECEC teacher education?
- **Method:** Summative content analysis of national regulations, guidelines, programme and course plans across 12 Norwegian ECTE institutions, framed by Goodlad's curriculum theory.
- **Findings:** Digital competence is emphasized at the ideological level but weakly at the formal and perceived levels.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — methodological twin abroad.** Direct methodological precedent for this project applied to Norwegian Infantil-equivalent. Goodlad's three-level curriculum frame is directly transferable to the Spanish setting (BOE / *memoria* / *guía docente*).

### A8. Instefjord, E. J., & Munthe, E. (2017). *Educating digitally competent teachers: A study of integration of professional digital competence in teacher education.* **Teaching and Teacher Education, 67**, 37–45. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** To what extent are professional digital competence competences integrated in Norwegian teacher-education curriculum documents?
- **Method:** Document analysis of curriculum documents from Norwegian teacher-education programmes.
- **Findings:** Little evidence of technology integration in the curriculum documents; persistent gap between policy and program design.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — methodological cornerstone for the project.** Listed in the domain profile as the methodological precedent. Enables the project to position itself as the Spanish counterpart, post-MRCDD.

### A9. Carrera, X., Coiduras, J. L., Lázaro-Cantabrana, J. L., et al. (2017). *El desarrollo de la competencia digital docente en los grados de Maestro/a de Educación Infantil y Primaria: descripción de una experiencia didáctica.* **(Various conference / journal proceedings — RELATEC / EDUTEC).** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION — title and authors confirmed via ResearchGate; venue uncertain]
- **Question:** How is TDC developed across the Infantil and Primaria *Grados* through a specific didactic experience?
- **Method:** Mixed didactic experience report; not a full curricular analysis but adjacent.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Tangential — implementation case rather than coverage map. Useful background for triangulation.

### A10. Esteve-Mon, F. M., Llopis-Nebot, M. A., & Adell-Segura, J. (2016). *Análisis de las competencias digitales de los graduados en titulaciones de maestro.* **RELATEC, 15**(2), 155–168. [VERIFIED]
- **Question:** What digital competences do graduates of Spanish *titulaciones de maestro* exhibit, and how do they relate to TPACK dimensions?
- **Method:** Self-report questionnaire grounded in TPACK with technology-specific items.
- **Sample:** 362 students from 14 Spanish universities (61 Infantil, 301 Primaria).
- **Findings:** Positive self-assessment of basic ICT and educational-tech knowledge; weaker on advanced pedagogical-technological integration.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Multi-university scope; key contextual baseline. The contribution is graduate-level survey rather than curricular analysis but the multi-university framing is directly relevant.

## Bucket B — Cross-sectional descriptive studies of TDC level among trainee teachers (saturated literature, useful for context)

### B1. Cabero-Almenara, J., Romero-Tena, R., Palacios-Rodríguez, A. (2020). *Evaluación de la competencia digital en futuros docentes: un análisis exploratorio en educación superior.* **EDUTEC, Revista Electrónica de Tecnología Educativa**. [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Cross-sectional survey using DigCompEdu-derived items; sample of pre-service teachers in higher education.
- **Findings:** Self-perceived TDC clusters around B1 level; weakest areas are evaluation and learner empowerment.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Methodologically saturated cross-section; useful as context anchor.

### B2. López-Belmonte, J., Pozo-Sánchez, S., Vázquez-Cano, E., & López-Meneses, E. J. (2020). *Análisis del impacto de la edad en la competencia digital del profesorado preuniversitario español.* **Pixel-Bit / Aloma / equivalent.** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Cross-sectional regression analyses of TDC by demographic variables.
- **Findings:** Age inversely correlated with self-rated TDC.
- **Proximity:** **2/5.** In-service rather than pre-service; useful background.

### B3. Esteve-Mon, F. M., Llopis-Nebot, M. A., & Adell-Segura, J. (2020). *Digital competence and computational thinking of student teachers.* **International Journal of Emerging Technologies in Learning (iJET).** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Cross-sectional survey, COMDID + CT items.
- **Findings:** Moderate self-perceived TDC; CT scores below TDC scores.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Strong author cluster on this project's exact topic.

### B4. Gutiérrez-Castillo, J. J., Cabero-Almenara, J., Estrada-Vidal, L. I. (2017). *Diseño y validación de un instrumento de evaluación de la competencia digital del estudiante.* **Pixel-Bit.** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Instrument development and validation among university students.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Methodological reference, instrument lineage.

### B5. Mercader, C., Ion, G., et al. (2024). *Digital competence profiles of first-year, pre-service teachers: Analysis in the Catalan university system.* **Teachers and Teaching, 31**(5). [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1080/13540602.2024.2313640]
- **Question:** What digital competence profiles characterize first-year pre-service teachers across the Catalan university system?
- **Method:** Cluster analysis of survey data; N = 947 first-year students from Catalan and Andorran universities.
- **Findings:** Three profiles — Confident (n = 525), Average (n = 311), Realistic (n = 111). Age and university-access route significantly differentiate profiles; gender does not.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Multi-institution Spanish (Catalan) sample focused on incoming students; directly complementary to a curricular analysis (you measure the curriculum, they measure the recipients).

### B6. Cabero-Almenara, J., Gutiérrez-Castillo, J. J., Palacios-Rodríguez, A., Barroso-Osuna, J. (2022). *La competencia digital docente. El caso de las universidades andaluzas.* **Aula Abierta, 51**(3). [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Survey of 2,262 university teachers in 9 Andalusian universities using adapted DigCompEdu Check-In.
- **Findings:** Moderate overall TDC; "digital pedagogy" and "digital resources" best evaluated.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** In-service university teachers, not pre-service students; methodological reference for the DigCompEdu Check-In adaptation.

### B7. Alastor, E., Guillén-Gámez, F. D., & Ruiz-Palmero, J. (2024). *Competencia digital del futuro docente de Educación Infantil y Primaria: un estudio por comparaciones múltiples.* **RELATEC, 23**(1), 9–24. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.17398/1695-288X.23.1.9]
- **Method:** Cross-sectional survey across Universidad de Málaga; comparative across degree (Infantil vs. Primaria) and academic course.
- **Findings:** Higher self-rated TDC for Primary than Infantil students; both medium-high. Significant differences across academic courses, especially within Primaria (all years differ except 3rd vs. 4th).
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Direct comparison of the two target degrees — the recipient-side analogue of the project's curricular comparison.

### B8. Aznar-Díaz, I., Fernández-Martín, F. D., Cáceres-Reche, M. del P., & Ayllón-Salas, P. (2026). *La competencia digital docente: un estudio en la formación inicial del profesorado de educación primaria.* **Bordón. Revista de Pedagogía, 78**(1), 173–189. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.13042/Bordon.2026.113106] *(Note: Round 1 listed this as Bordón 76(3) 2024; corrected to 78(1) 2026 after publisher-page verification.)*
- **Method:** Cross-sectional survey of Primary trainee teachers.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Adds Bordón visibility to the saturated literature; recent (forthcoming print).

### B9. Méndez-Aguado, C., et al. (2020). *Análisis de la autopercepción sobre el nivel de competencia digital docente en la formación inicial de maestros/as.* **Revista Electrónica Interuniversitaria de Formación del Profesorado.** [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Online questionnaire, INTEF Common Framework; N = 117 4th-year students at the Faculty of Education in Albacete.
- **Findings:** Basic level on most areas, both at start and end of degree.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Single-faculty convenience sample; representative of the saturated cross-sectional literature.

### B10. Pérez-Escoda, A., García-Ruiz, R., Aguaded, I. (2018). *La competencia digital docente como clave para fortalecer el uso responsable de Internet.* **Campus Virtuales, 7**(1). [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Reflective and survey-based.
- **Proximity:** **2/5.** Tangential — TDC framing for safe use of Internet.

### B11. Pérez Garcias, A., Darder Mesquida, A., de-Benito Crosetti, B., & Negre-Bennasar, F. (2024). *La competencia digital y la agencia digital docente en la formación inicial del profesorado.* **Bordón. Revista de Pedagogía, 76**(2), 27–44. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.13042/Bordon.2024.100546]
- **Method:** Conceptual + survey work introducing the construct of "digital teacher agency"; sample of Primary education students at the Universitat de les Illes Balears.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Same population, novel construct (agency) that could be triangulated against curricular coverage.

### B12. Moreno Fuentes, E., Hidalgo Navarrete, J., & Risueño Martínez, J. J. (2025). *Evaluación de la competencia digital en futuros docentes: un análisis basado en el marco europeo DigCompEdu.* **European Public & Social Innovation Review, 10**, 1–18. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.31637/epsir-2025-2450] *(Round 1 listed this as Revista Tecnología, Ciencia y Educación; corrected after verification.)*
- **Method:** Survey of 179 students at Centro Universitario Sagrada Familia (Universidad de Jaén, Úbeda) in academic year 2023–24, using DigCompEdu-adapted instrument.
- **Findings:** Students at B1 level across the six DigCompEdu areas; significant differences by course year, no gender effect.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.**

### B13. Fernández-Cruz, F. J., & Fernández-Díaz, M. J. (2016). *Los docentes de la Generación Z y sus competencias digitales.* **Comunicar, 24**(46), 97–105. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.3916/C46-2016-10] *(Tier 1 added in Round 2.)*
- **Question:** What level of ICT competence do Spanish primary and secondary teachers exhibit, framed against UNESCO 2008/2011 ICT competence standards?
- **Method:** Cross-sectional survey of 1,433 teachers in 80 schools in the Madrid region.
- **Findings:** Teachers report intermediate ICT competence overall; pedagogical-digital integration trails technical literacy. The article foregrounds the gap between Gen-Z students' digital culture and teachers' competence.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** In-service rather than pre-service; included as the most-cited Spanish cross-sectional anchor in the TDC literature — required reference for the introduction's contextual framing.

## Bucket C — Intervention / training studies in initial teacher training

### C1. Ramírez-Verdugo, M. D., et al. (2025). *Use of Instagram as an educational tool with pre-service teachers and the impact on digital competence in communication and collaboration.* **Education Sciences, 15**(2), 149. [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Quasi-experimental pre-post; N = 391 students at the University of Granada; 11-week Instagram intervention.
- **Findings:** Intermediate TDC at both pre and post, with significant gains post-intervention.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Method-context match (intervention in Spanish initial teacher training).

### C2. Marín-Suelves, D., et al. (2023). *Digital competence of training teachers: Results of a teaching innovation project.* **Education Sciences, 13**(2), 162. [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Pre-post evaluation of a teaching-innovation project at the University of Valencia (Infantil + Primaria degrees).
- **Findings:** Improvement across all DigCompEdu areas; gender differences detected.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Same target population (Infantil + Primaria, Spanish public university).

### C3. Esteve-Mon, F. M., Llopis-Nebot, M. A., et al. (2020). *Improving future teachers' digital competence using active methodologies.* **Sustainability, 12**(18), 7798. [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Active-methodologies intervention with Spanish pre-service teachers.
- **Findings:** Positive effect on TDC.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Useful complementary intervention evidence by the central author cluster.

## Bucket D — Framework / measurement papers (DigCompEdu, MRCDD, TPACK, COMDID)

### D1. Redecker, C., & Punie, Y. (2017). **European Framework for the Digital Competence of Educators: DigCompEdu.** Publications Office of the European Union, JRC107466. [VERIFIED]
- The official EU JRC report. 22 competences across 6 areas; CEFR-style A1–C2 progression.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Required cite — the framework itself.

### D2. Punie, Y., & Redecker, C. (2017). **DigCompEdu Check-In Self-Reflection Tool.** [VERIFIED]
- The 22-item self-assessment instrument operationalizing D1.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Required cite if the instrument enters the project.

### D3. Caena, F., & Redecker, C. (2019). *Aligning teacher competence frameworks to 21st-century challenges: The case for the European Digital Competence Framework for Educators (DigCompEdu).* **European Journal of Education, 54**(3), 356–369. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1111/ejed.12345]
- Conceptual articulation of DigCompEdu and rationale for cross-context applicability.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Frame the framework.

### D4. Cabero-Almenara, J., Romero-Tena, R., Barroso-Osuna, J., Palacios-Rodríguez, A. (2020). *Development of the teacher digital competence: Validation of DigCompEdu Check-In questionnaire in the university context of Andalusia (Spain).* **Sustainability, 12**(15), 6094. [VERIFIED]
- **Method:** Spanish translation, adaptation, and validation of DigCompEdu Check-In; N = 2,262 Andalusian university teachers.
- **Findings:** High reliability and validity overall and by dimension.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Spanish-language validated instrument; required if the project uses any Check-In derivative.

### D5. Lázaro-Cantabrana, J. L., Usart-Rodríguez, M., & Gisbert-Cervera, M. (2019). *Assessing teacher digital competence: The construction of an instrument for measuring the knowledge of pre-service teachers.* **JNE / Educación XX1 / equivalent — see references.bib.** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- **Method:** Construction and validation of the COMDID instrument family (COMDID-A self-report, COMDID-C performance).
- **Findings:** Four-dimension structure: didactic-curricular-methodological; planning/management of digital resources; ethical/legal/security; personal/professional.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Spanish-context, pre-service-specific instrument; the natural alternative to DigCompEdu Check-In for Spanish teacher education.

### D6. Lázaro, J. L., & Gisbert, M. (2015). *Elaboración de una rúbrica para evaluar la competencia digital del docente.* **UT. Revista de Ciències de l'Educació.** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- The original rubric underlying COMDID.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.**

### D7. Mishra, P., & Koehler, M. J. (2006). *Technological pedagogical content knowledge: A framework for teacher knowledge.* **Teachers College Record, 108**(6), 1017–1054. [VERIFIED]
- The original TPACK framework.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Required foundational cite for any pedagogical-digital integration argument.

### D8. Koehler, M. J., & Mishra, P. (2009). *What is technological pedagogical content knowledge?* **Contemporary Issues in Technology and Teacher Education, 9**(1), 60–70. [VERIFIED]
- TPACK clarified and expanded.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.**

### D9. Falloon, G. (2020). *From digital literacy to digital competence: The teacher digital competency (TDC) framework.* **Educational Technology Research and Development, 68**(5), 2449–2472. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1007/s11423-020-09767-4]
- Holistic TDC framework extending TPACK with personal-ethical and personal-professional dimensions.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** A leading current TDC conceptualization in the international literature.

### D10. Krumsvik, R. J. (2014). *Teacher educators' digital competence.* **Scandinavian Journal of Educational Research, 58**(3), 269–280. [VERIFIED]
- Foundational TDC conceptualization (cognitive, metacognitive, learning strategies, self-efficacy, didactic methods in digital environments).
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Required by domain profile.

### D11. Gudmundsdottir, G. B., & Hatlevik, O. E. (2018). *Newly qualified teachers' professional digital competence: Implications for teacher education.* **European Journal of Teacher Education, 41**(2), 214–231. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1080/02619768.2017.1416085]
- **Method:** Nationwide Norwegian survey, N = 356 newly qualified teachers.
- **Findings:** ITE contribution to PDC perceived as low; ICT self-efficacy correlates with perceived ITE quality. Three-part taxonomy: generic / subject-matter / profession-related digital competence.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Comparative Norwegian benchmark recommended in domain profile.

### D12. INTEF (2017). **Marco Común de Competencia Digital Docente.** Ministerio de Educación. [VERIFIED]
- The first formal Spanish TDC framework, superseded by D13.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Historical context.

### D13. INTEF (2022). **Marco de Referencia de la Competencia Digital Docente (MRCDD).** Ministerio de Educación y Formación Profesional. [VERIFIED]
- The current Spanish TDC framework: 6 areas, 23 competences, 6 progression stages (A1–C2). Foundational for any Spanish-context study.
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Required cite.

### D14. Ghomi, M., & Redecker, C. (2019). *Digital Competence of Educators (DigCompEdu): Development and evaluation of a self-assessment instrument for teachers' digital competence.* **CSEDU 2019.** [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- Original validation study of the DigCompEdu Check-In on a European sample.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.**

### D15. Schmidt, D. A., Baran, E., Thompson, A. D., et al. (2009). *Technological pedagogical content knowledge (TPACK): The development and validation of an assessment instrument for preservice teachers.* **Journal of Research on Technology in Education, 42**(2), 123–149. [VERIFIED]
- The TPACK self-assessment instrument.
- **Proximity:** **3/5.** Useful if any TPACK measurement enters the project.

### D16. Lázaro-Cantabrana, J. L., Gisbert-Cervera, M., & Silva-Quiroz, J. (2018). *Una rúbrica para evaluar la competencia digital del profesor universitario en el contexto latinoamericano.* **Edutec. Revista Electrónica de Tecnología Educativa, (63)**. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.21556/edutec.2018.63.1091] *(Tier 1 added in Round 2.)*
- **Method:** Adaptation of the COMDID/Lázaro-Gisbert (2015) rubric to the Iberoamerican higher-education context via focus groups; 22 descriptors, 4 dimensions, 4 development levels.
- **Findings:** Validates the trans-Atlantic transferability of the COMDID rubric; standard reference for any Latin American or Iberoamerican TDC measurement.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.** Iberoamerican COMDID benchmark — fills the depth gap in the Lázaro-Cantabrana cluster flagged by the librarian-critic.

## Bucket E — Foundational / theoretical references

### E1. Tondeur, J., van Braak, J., Sang, G., Voogt, J., Fisser, P., & Ottenbreit-Leftwich, A. (2012). *Preparing pre-service teachers to integrate technology in education: A synthesis of qualitative evidence.* **Computers & Education, 59**(1), 134–144. [VERIFIED]
- Original SQD model — six strategies (role modelling, reflection, instructional design, collaboration, authentic experience, continuous feedback).
- **Proximity:** **5/5.** Foundational; required cite for ITE design arguments.

### E2. Tondeur, J., et al. (2017). *Preparing beginning teachers for technology integration in education: Ready for take-off?* **Technology, Pedagogy and Education, 26**(2), 157–177. [PARTIAL VERIFICATION]
- SQD model applied to beginning teachers.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.**

### E3. Tondeur, J., Pareja-Roblin, N., van Braak, J., Voogt, J., & Prestridge, S. (2019). *Teacher educators as gatekeepers: Preparing the next generation of teachers for technology integration in education.* **British Journal of Educational Technology, 50**(3), 1189–1209. [VERIFIED]
- Role of teacher educators in mediating ITE TDC outcomes.
- **Proximity:** **4/5.**

### E4. Tondeur, J., Howard, S., Van Zanten, M., et al. (2025). *Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies.* **Computers & Education**. [VERIFIED — recent update of the SQD model]

## Bucket E.M — Methods (content analysis, intercoder reliability) — *NEW sub-bucket added in Round 2*

This sub-bucket holds the methodological grammar required by the strategist to design the project's coding scheme and intercoder-reliability protocol. The domain profile mandates Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70 (Landis & Koch); these references provide the methodological warrant.

### E.M1. Hsieh, H. F., & Shannon, S. E. (2005). *Three approaches to qualitative content analysis.* **Qualitative Health Research, 15**(9), 1277–1288. [VERIFIED — DOI 10.1177/1049732305276687]
- **Question:** How do conventional, directed, and summative content analysis differ in coding-scheme construction, inferential goals, and trustworthiness criteria?
- **Method:** Methodological article distinguishing three approaches to qualitative content analysis: (i) conventional (codes inductive from data); (ii) directed (codes deductive from prior theory); (iii) summative (codes count manifest content, then interpret latent meaning).
- **Why it matters here:** A7 (Sandvik et al., 2023) — the methodological twin abroad — uses *summative* content analysis. The project will use *directed* content analysis (DigCompEdu and MRCDD as a priori coding frame applied to *memorias* and *guías docentes*). Hsieh & Shannon supplies the canonical justification for choosing directed over conventional or summative when an established framework exists.
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — methodological backbone.** Without this reference, the strategist cannot defend the coding-scheme design.

### E.M2. Krippendorff, K. (2018). *Content Analysis: An Introduction to Its Methodology* (4th ed.). SAGE Publications. [VERIFIED — book]
- **Topic:** The standard textbook on content analysis methodology, covering unitization, sampling, coding, intercoder reliability (Krippendorff's α and Cohen's κ), and validation.
- **Why it matters here:** The domain profile mandates Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70 for intercoder reliability without naming a source. Krippendorff supplies the canonical reference for (a) the choice of κ vs. α, (b) the ≥ 0.70 threshold (consistent with Landis & Koch 1977), (c) procedures for handling coder disagreement, and (d) the unit-of-analysis question that pervades curricular document analysis (paragraph vs. competence statement vs. course-level aggregate).
- **Proximity:** **5/5 — methodological backbone.** Required reference for the project's reliability argument.
