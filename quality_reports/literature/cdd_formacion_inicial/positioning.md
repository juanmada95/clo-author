# Positioning — TDC in Initial Teacher Training (Spain, Infantil + Primaria)

**Project:** Curricular analysis of TDC formation in *Grado en Maestro de Educación Infantil* and *Grado en Maestro de Educación Primaria*, mapped against DigCompEdu and the MRCDD.
**Companion files:** `annotated_bibliography.md`, `frontier_map.md`.
**Date:** 2026-04-26.

---

## 1. Suggested contribution statement (one paragraph)

> This study provides the first **post-MRCDD, two-degree, two-layer** curricular analysis of how the Spanish *Grado en Maestro de Educación Infantil* and *Grado en Maestro de Educación Primaria* develop teacher digital competence after the entry into force of the *Marco de Referencia de la Competencia Digital Docente* (MRCDD; Resolución 4 de mayo de 2022). Each qualifier is defensible against a named existing precedent; the conjunction is the contribution. Building on the methodological precedent of Instefjord and Munthe (2017) for Norway and the curricular-levels frame of Sandvik, Smørdal and Østerud (2023), we code the *memorias de verificación* (formal curriculum) and the *guías docentes* (perceived curriculum) of [N] Spanish universities against the six areas and twenty-three competences of the MRCDD, benchmarked against DigCompEdu (Redecker & Punie, 2017). We document the extent and depth of TDC integration, identify systematic gaps between policy framework and curricular implementation, and contrast the formal-level coverage with the more granular *guía docente* layer. The study addresses the gap left by prior Spanish curricular work that has been restricted by subject area (Cuevas-Monzonís et al., 2024, 2025), by competence subset (Sanz-Benito et al., 2024), or by curricular layer (Peirats et al., 2018; Granados et al., 2020) — and complements the saturated descriptive literature on trainee teachers' self-perceived TDC by mapping the curricular *supply* against which that demand is observed.

## 3. Differentiation against the methodological precedents

| Precedent | What it does | What we do similarly / differently |
|---|---|---|
| **Instefjord & Munthe (2017)** — Norway, full ITE, document analysis | Codes Norwegian teacher-education curriculum documents for digital competence integration | **Same design family**, applied to the Spanish post-MRCDD setting; methodological precedent named in the introduction |
| **Sandvik, Smørdal & Østerud (2023)** — Norway, ECEC, Goodlad three-level frame | Distinguishes ideological / formal / perceived curricula across 12 institutions | **We adopt the Goodlad scaffold** and translate it into the Spanish institutional layers (BOE/LOMLOE → *memoria de verificación* → *guía docente*) |

## 4. Differentiation against the saturated descriptive literature (Bucket B)

The Bucket B literature (B1–B12) measures **what trainee teachers report** about their TDC. It does not measure **what the curriculum delivers**. Our contribution is to map the **supply side**, against which Bucket B's findings can be reinterpreted: a documented curricular gap in (e.g.) MRCDD Area 4 (Evaluation and Feedback) is consistent with the recurring Bucket B finding that this area is the weakest in trainee self-perception (B1, B3, B12). The project therefore positions itself **complementarily**, not as a substitute, to the dominant publication mode in the field.

## 5. Two-sentence elevator pitch (for use in the abstract and the introduction)

> Spain has a teacher-digital-competence reference framework (MRCDD, 2022) but no post-MRCDD evidence at the curricular level on whether initial-training programmes for future Infantil and Primary teachers actually deliver it across both layers (formal *memoria* and perceived *guía docente*). We map the formal and perceived curricula of a stratified random sample of ~60 Spanish degree programmes — drawn from the population of ~144 *Grado en Maestro de Educación Infantil* and *Grado en Maestro de Educación Primaria* programmes — against the MRCDD's six areas and twenty-three competences, identify systematic gaps, and discuss what those gaps imply for the design of digitally competent teacher preparation.

## 7. Risks to manage in the framing

- **Do not overclaim "first."** Frame as **"first post-MRCDD, two-degree, two-layer stratified analysis"** — each qualifier is defensible against an existing competitor; the conjunction is the contribution. Avoid the unqualified "first comprehensive" wording: the design is a stratified sample of ~60 programmes (Tier B per data discovery 2026-04-26), not a census.
- **Acknowledge the Cuevas-Monzonís and Sanz-Benito precedents explicitly in the introduction.** Pretending they don't exist is the fastest route to a desk reject from a Spanish-context journal.
- **Defend the geographic scope.** The Tier B stratified sample (~60 programmes from the universe of ~144) is justified against the population of *Grado en Maestro* providers via stratified random sampling on sector × autonomous community × modality, with *centros adscritos* as a separate sector cell. The pilot retrieval-rate evidence (Wilson 95% CI [24%, 76%] on Layer 2) made a census infeasible; this is documented in `quality_reports/data_exploration_cdd_formacion_inicial.md`.
- **Defend the layer choice.** *Memoria de verificación* + *guía docente* is a deliberate design that buys analytical leverage; the *memoria* alone is the formal commitment, the *guía* is the unit-of-practice commitment, and the gap between them is itself a finding.

## 8. What the strategist takes from this

- Adopt **MRCDD as the primary coding grid**, DigCompEdu as European benchmark.
- Adopt **Goodlad's three-level curriculum frame** (E6, A7) as the analytical scaffold; instantiate as BOE → *memoria* → *guía docente*.
- Anchor the design in **Instefjord & Munthe (2017, A8)** and **Sandvik et al. (2023, A7)** as methodological precedents.
- Differentiate explicitly against **Cuevas-Monzonís et al. (2024, 2025)**, **Sanz-Benito et al. (2024)**, and **Peirats et al. (2018) / Granados et al. (2020)** in the introduction.
- Position complementarily — not in opposition — to the saturated descriptive literature (Bucket B).
- Initial target journal: **Educación XX1**.
