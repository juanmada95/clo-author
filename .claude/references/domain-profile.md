**Geographic focus:** Spain (primary), European Union, Latin America (secondary)
**Educational level studied:** Undergraduate degrees in Early Childhood Education (*Grado en Maestro de Educación Infantil*) and Primary Education (*Grado en Maestro de Educación Primaria*)

## Common Identification / Research Strategies

<!-- The Strategist considers these first. The strategist-critic knows field-specific threats. -->

| Strategy | Typical Application | Key Assumption / Threat |
|----------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Curricular / document analysis | Mapping competence coverage in degree syllabi | Coding scheme validity; intercoder reliability (Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70); selection of which documents count (memoria vs. guía docente vs. actual practice) |
| Cross-sectional survey | Self-perceived TDC level among trainee teachers | Self-report bias; convenience sampling; common-method variance |
| Pre-post / quasi-experimental | Evaluating effectiveness of a training intervention | Lack of control group; maturation; testing effects; small N |
| Mixed-methods (QUAN + QUAL) | Triangulating self-report with focus groups / interviews | Integration logic; sample alignment between strands |
| Bibliometric / systematic review (PRISMA) | Mapping the field; identifying gaps | Database coverage (WoS vs. Scopus vs. ERIC); language bias |
| Comparative across institutions / countries | Differences in TDC training between universities or systems | Comparability of programs; institutional confounders |

**For this project (curricular analysis angle):** primary candidates are document analysis of *memorias de verificación* and *guías docentes*, supplemented by bibliometric mapping of how prior literature has analyzed teacher training curricula.

## Field Conventions

<!-- The Coder and Writer follow these. The writer-critic checks for them. -->

- **Citation style:** APA 7th edition (NOT Chicago, AEA, or Harvard). Spanish journals also use APA but with Spanish-language conventions ("y" instead of "&" in narrative citations).
- **Frameworks must be cited explicitly:** DigCompEdu → cite Redecker & Punie (2017); MRCDD → cite Resolución 4 mayo 2022 (BOE) and INTEF (2022); TPACK → cite Mishra & Koehler (2006) and Koehler & Mishra (2009).
- **Distinguish carefully:** *digital literacy* (general user skills) vs. *teacher digital competence* (pedagogical-digital integration). These are NOT synonyms.
- **Self-perception vs. performance:** Always state explicitly whether the measure is self-reported (most common) or performance-based (rare). Do not generalize from one to the other.
- **Instrument validity:** Cite validation evidence (Cronbach's α, CFA fit indices) for every instrument used. If using DigCompEdu, specify the version.
- **Sample reporting:** Report N, gender distribution, age, course year, university/faculty, and sampling method. Convenience samples are the norm but must be acknowledged as a limitation.
- **Effect size:** Report Cohen's *d*, η², or equivalent alongside p-values. For ordinal/Likert outcomes, use non-parametric alternatives where appropriate.
- **Visualizations:** Bar charts for categorical comparisons; radar/spider plots conventional for displaying TDC across the 6 DigCompEdu areas; heatmaps for curricular coverage matrices.
- **Language consideration:** Spanish journals expect a bilingual abstract (Spanish + English) and bilingual keywords.

## Field-Specific Referee Concerns

<!-- The domain-referee and methods-referee watch for these. -->

- **"What's the difference between digital literacy and TDC?"** — Reviewers will reject papers that conflate the two.
- **"Is this self-report or performance-based?"** — If self-report, social desirability bias must be discussed.
- **"Which version of DigCompEdu / MRCDD did you use, and why?"** — The framework has evolved; specify and justify.
- **"What is the sample frame and how was it selected?"** — Convenience samples are acceptable but must be acknowledged.
- **"Did you report instrument reliability and validity?"** — Cronbach's α at minimum; CFA preferred for new adaptations.
- **"What's the contribution beyond yet another descriptive study?"** — The field is saturated with cross-sectional self-report studies; novelty bar is rising.
- **"Is the curricular analysis based on the *memoria de verificación*, the *guía docente*, or actual classroom practice?"** — These three levels can diverge substantially; the unit of analysis matters.
- **"How do you handle the gap between the official curriculum and the implemented curriculum?"** — Classic curriculum studies critique; expected acknowledgment.
- **"What about the digital divide / equity dimension?"** — Increasingly expected, especially post-COVID.
- **"Why analyze only Spanish degrees?"** — Need to defend the geographic scope or include a comparative element.

## Quality Tolerance Thresholds

<!-- Customize for your domain's standards. Used by quality.md. -->

| Quantity | Tolerance | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Cronbach's α (instrument reliability) | ≥ 0.70 acceptable, ≥ 0.80 preferred | Standard psychometric thresholds |
| CFA fit indices | CFI ≥ 0.90, RMSEA ≤ 0.08, SRMR ≤ 0.08 | Hu & Bentler (1999) conventions |
| Intercoder reliability (qualitative coding) | Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70, ≥ 0.80 preferred | Landis & Koch (1977) thresholds |
| Effect size reporting | Cohen's *d*, η², or r in addition to p-values | APA 7 reporting standards |
| Sample size justification | A priori power analysis for inferential studies; saturation rationale for qualitative | Editorial expectation in BJET, C&E, Comunicar |
